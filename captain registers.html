Welcome to @league_name! We’re glad to have you around for another exciting season of ultimate! Before you hit the field, there are a few more steps to complete registration:

PAYMENTS

If you paid a deposit, the balance is due when registration closes. Teams that don't pay the balance on time will not be eligible for play. See outstanding balances at vul.ca/myvul.

Tip: when collecting money from your team, consider asking for an extra $5 to cover unforeseen costs, such as default penalties or a player that cancels.

ROSTER

Teams must have at least 12 players on their roster by the close of registration. Teams that don't will lose their place in the priority list and may not get their preferred timeslot if it fills up. To complete your roster, find your team page at vul.ca/myvul and click "Edit Roster".

  • Teams must have at least one woman-matching captain or assistant captain.
  • Teams must have at least one Rule Guru. This can be a captain or a regular player.
  • If you’re looking for players, post in Matchmaker.

CAPTAINS’ MEETING

All teams must send a representative to the online captains’ meeting. If you cannot attend, please arrange for an assistant captain or teammate to join. Information about this meeting will be sent out later.

MEMBERSHIP PERKS

As a Vancouver Ultimate member, you get:

  • Discounts on jerseys, apparel, discs, and gloves
  • Discounts at local restaurants, bars, and service providers
  • Access to our Sport Accident Insurance policy.
